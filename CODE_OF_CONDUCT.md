# Code of Conduct

The ETIS Engineering Platform is committed to a professional, respectful, inclusive, and constructive collaboration environment.

Engineering work benefits from rigorous review, evidence-based disagreement, and thoughtful challenge. Those practices should strengthen the work without diminishing the people participating in it.

## Our Standard

Participants are expected to:

- communicate professionally and respectfully;
- critique ideas, assumptions, evidence, designs, and artifacts rather than people;
- welcome evidence-based disagreement and constructive technical challenge;
- explain reasoning when challenging an engineering position;
- assume good faith while still reviewing work rigorously;
- acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes when evidence changes;
- respect differences in experience, background, perspective, and expertise;
- avoid harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or demeaning conduct;
- protect confidential, private, and sensitive information;
- use project spaces for legitimate engineering, educational, and community purposes; and
- preserve the engineering integrity and professional purpose of the project.

Professional disagreement is expected.

Disagreement becomes unacceptable when it shifts from challenging the engineering work to attacking, intimidating, dismissing, or targeting the person presenting it.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments;
- harassment, intimidation, threats, or deliberate humiliation;
- discriminatory language or conduct;
- unwelcome sexual attention or inappropriate personal conduct;
- sustained disruption of technical, educational, or community discussions;
- deliberate misrepresentation of another participant's work or position;
- knowingly submitting misleading, malicious, or harmful content;
- misuse of project, issue, pull request, Discussion, or community spaces;
- publishing another person's private or sensitive information without authorization;
- attempting to expose credentials, private repositories, confidential materials, or protected data; and
- retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern.

## Engineering Review and Disagreement

ETIS encourages rigorous engineering review.

Participants may strongly challenge:

- assumptions;
- requirements;
- architecture;
- implementation choices;
- evidence;
- AI-generated material;
- governance decisions;
- risk assessments;
- release-readiness claims; and
- other engineering conclusions.

Such challenges should remain grounded in evidence, engineering reasoning, relevant constraints, or clearly stated professional judgment.

A request for evidence, a critical technical review, or a professional disagreement is not itself a conduct violation merely because it is direct or challenges an established position.

The goal is better engineering judgment, not artificial consensus.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to participation in project-managed spaces associated with the ETIS Engineering Platform, including:

- repository Issues;
- pull requests and code reviews;
- GitHub Discussions;
- contribution and maintenance activities;
- project documentation collaboration; and
- other project-managed communication spaces.

Participants representing the project in an official capacity are also expected to follow these standards.

## Reporting Conduct Concerns

Conduct concerns should be reported privately to the project maintainers rather than through a public Issue or Discussion when doing so would expose personal, sensitive, or potentially harmful information.

Include only the information necessary to explain the concern.

Do not use security vulnerability reporting channels for ordinary conduct matters unless the concern also involves a genuine security issue.

Reports will be handled as privately and fairly as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the nature and severity of the behavior, maintainers may:

- provide a private warning;
- request that content or behavior be corrected;
- edit, hide, or remove inappropriate content;
- reject or close contributions;
- restrict participation in project spaces; or
- take other reasonable actions necessary to protect the project and its participants.

Repeated, deliberate, or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions on participation.

Maintainers should apply enforcement proportionately and in good faith.

## Engineering Integrity

Professional collaboration and engineering rigor are complementary.

ETIS depends on people being willing to question assumptions, expose weak evidence, identify risk, challenge conclusions, and change their position when the evidence warrants it.

The standard is therefore not agreement.

The standard is **professional, evidence-centered engineering discourse conducted with respect for the people involved.**
