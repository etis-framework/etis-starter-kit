## ETIS Framework and Engineering Platform

The ETIS Framework defines an engineering discipline for building trustworthy intelligent systems.

The Engineering Platform provides reusable guidance, Engineering Stages, templates, reference examples, governance assets, and Project Workspace patterns for applying that discipline.

Use of the Engineering Platform does not imply that a system has been certified, approved, endorsed, verified, or determined to be secure, compliant, safe, trustworthy, or ready for operation.

Engineering teams remain responsible for project-specific decisions, evidence, verification, governance, risk acceptance, and professional judgment.

## Reference Examples

Lakeside Metropolitan University (LMU) and the Campus Operations and Incident Coordination Platform (COICP) are fictional reference examples used to demonstrate ETIS engineering practices.

They are provided for instructional and professional reference purposes.

Reference examples illustrate possible engineering approaches. They do not establish requirements for another project and do not constitute project-specific engineering evidence.

## ETIS Doctrine

Core ETIS engineering principles reflected throughout this repository include:

- **AI proposes; engineers verify.**
- **Governance is architecture.**
- **Context is control.**
- **Everything important leaves evidence.**
- **The model is not the system.**
- **A demo is not operational proof.**

These principles inform the Engineering Platform's lifecycle guidance, templates, reference examples, governance assets, readiness expectations, and repository-centered engineering practices.
